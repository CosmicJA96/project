The Solar System

The solar system is a star and planetary system consisting of a single star, 8 major planets, 5 dwarf planets and nearly 900 known moons. There are 4 rocky planets in the inner solar system: Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars. And 4 giant planets in the outer solar system: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. There are also dwarf planets in the Solar System, but we won't go through them. There will be more discoveries in the future, but who knows?
